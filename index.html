Catch GST invoice errors before they reach the books.

Reads Indian GST invoices from PDF or phone photo, puts the key fields in a spreadsheet, and flags the invoices that look wrong instead of guessing.

What it checks on every invoice

Each invoice is marked OK, CHECK or REVIEW, and the Excel file colours the rows to match.

GSTIN validity

Format, state code and checksum, which catches typing and OCR mistakes.

Totals add up

Taxable value plus tax must match the invoice total, within rupee rounding.

CGST equals SGST

The two halves are always equal, so any difference is flagged.

Right tax type

IGST versus CGST and SGST is checked against the states of supplier and buyer.

Standard rate

The implied rate is compared with the 0, 3, 5, 12, 18 and 28 percent slabs.

How it works

1. Upload

PDF invoices or scans and photos. Scans are read with OCR.

2. Extract

Supplier, GSTINs, invoice number, date, taxable value, taxes and total.

3. Review

Open the spreadsheet and look only at the rows marked CHECK or REVIEW.

Free pilot for CA firms

This is an early version. I would like to run it on 5 to 10 of your real invoices, free, and show you the output. Redacted copies are fine, and I delete the files once we have reviewed the results.

Known limits today: line items and HSN codes are not extracted, unusual layouts may need a manual check, and the output is a generic spreadsheet, not a Tally import file. It is a data-entry aid, not tax advice.
